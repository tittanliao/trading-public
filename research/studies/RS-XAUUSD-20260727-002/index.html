Key findings

V3.9 outperforms V3.4 on the same overall metrics

WR 56.22% vs 53.17%, PF 1.869 vs 1.525, despite fewer trades (450 vs 504) over a similar span. Consistent with V3.9 being the promoted successor version.

30-minute-slot deltas are noisy at this sample size

Most of the 48 slots have single-digit-to-teens counts per version; win-rate differences up to ±35 percentage points appear at low n (e.g. 14:30, 15:30, 16:30) and should not be read as a stable timing edge for either version.

Exit-rule differences confound the comparison

V3.4 used 0.5% SL with TP2=2R; V3.9's exit structure differs. A slot or session performing better under V3.9 may reflect the exit-rule change rather than a timing/regime effect.

Method now normalizes both TradingView export schemas

V3.4 exports use 'Trade #'/'Net P&L USD'/'Net P&L %'; V3.9 exports use 'Trade number'/'Net PnL USD'/'Return %'. The runner maps both to one canonical schema so results are directly comparable.

Method and evidence boundary

V3.4 and V3.9 differ in exit-rule structure (0.5% SL / TP2=2R vs V3.9's current exit rules) and in which market regime each was traded in, so raw 30-minute-slot performance is expected to differ and must not be read as a like-for-like backtest of the same rules across time.
