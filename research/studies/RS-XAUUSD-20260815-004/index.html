Key findings

Waiting one bar is not sufficient; delayed market entry loses edge

The candle-level exit emulator matched all 472 OFF TradingView exit timestamps and IDs. After T1 preserved the signal low, market entry produced 336 trades at 54.46% WR, PF 1.588, and USD 13.17 average PnL, below the exact same-source OFF entries at 61.31% WR, PF 2.308, and USD 24.84 average PnL.

0.15% pullback is a low-coverage shadow candidate, not an adopted rule

The frozen 0.10% primary pullback weakened to 46.67% WR in the recent cohort and is rejected. The post-output 0.15% sensitivity filled 77/336 T1-held opportunities (22.92%) at 59.74% WR, PF 2.106, and USD 21.79 average PnL; recent n=39 remained positive at 53.85% WR and PF 1.836. Its 95% WR interval is wide (48.58–69.98%) and the paired outcome test versus same-source OFF is non-significant (p=1.0), so it is shadow-only.

Evidence limits

  • The signal cohort is frozen to 472 OFF filled opportunities; it cannot recover qualified signals that the formal strategy did not fill while already in position.
  • The 0.15% pullback was selected after reviewing the sensitivity output, fills only 77 signals, and requires prospective shadow validation.
  • The 0.15% win-rate confidence interval is wide and its paired outcome test versus same-source OFF is not significant.
  • No result changes formal S1 logic, live risk, or an active entry checklist.

Method and evidence boundary

Waiting alone will not create edge; a sufficiently patient pullback may improve entry geometry but must remain stable and retain enough fills.

All timestamps use Asia/Taipei. Raw CSV, per-trade output, source manifests, and private decision records are not published. The Python method accepts locally authorized inputs and reproduces the reviewed aggregate.
