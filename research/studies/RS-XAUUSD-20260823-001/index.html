Key findings

The intraday range profile is real, and it is not the arcsine of a random walk

Against a null that shuffles each session's own bar-to-bar changes and rebuilds the path — preserving that day's realised volatility, its bar count and the arcsine geometry — the observed profile's family permutation p is 0.005 in all three periods, the resolution floor at 200 shuffles. The largest |z| is 9.07 on train and 4.62 on holdout against a null median near 1.9. Thirty-two of 48 half-hour slots are sign-consistent across all three periods against 12 expected by chance, and 10 of them clear |z|>2 in every period.

Ten half hours carry consistently more or less range than chance

More: 09:00 Taipei (z 7.07 / 5.54 / 5.92), and the block 20:30 through 23:00. Less: 10:00, 12:00 and 12:30, the Taipei lunch window. 09:00 is the single strongest non-boundary slot at 7.32% of the average day's range. This is the intraday volatility profile at 30-minute resolution, and it says nothing about direction.

The busiest US half hour moves one hour twice a year, and a fixed Taipei clock is wrong by 5x when it does

The peak of the US block is 08:30 ET, the standard macro release time. On the Taipei clock that is 20:30 when US DST is on and 21:30 when it is off. Taipei 20:30 carries 4.51% of the day's range during DST and 0.88% outside it — a factor of five in the same clock slot. Aligning the US window to the New York clock sharpens its peak (4.81% vs 4.43%). Any profile read off a fixed Taipei clock averages two different markets for a third of the year.

The shape is stable and the level is not

Increment Spearman across periods is 0.877 to 0.919 and every weekday reproduces the same shape at 0.91 to 0.97. But the share completed by 09:30 rose from 27.2% in 2024Q1 to 39.5% in 2026Q3, Spearman 0.873 against time, and the largest level gap between train and holdout is 12.23 points. A threshold memorised from the pooled profile understates the morning by roughly a fifth of the day's range.

The rise in the Asian share is a time trend, not a volatility artefact, and the obvious test says the opposite

Quarterly Asian share correlates with quarterly mean range at Spearman 0.936, which reads as the volatility regime explaining everything — gold's daily range went from $25 to $174 over the sample. Sorting sessions by their own range instead of by date reverses it: inside the first half of the sample, large-range days complete 28.1% by 09:30 against 35.9% for small-range days, and inside the second half 38.8% against 42.6%. The sign flips in both halves, so the pooled correlation is Simpson's paradox and the calendar trend survives.

A busy Asian morning does not mean more is coming

Sorted into terciles by morning range against the trailing 20-session median, a busy morning goes with a bigger full day (median ratio 1.234 against 0.888) at Spearman 0.369 — but the rest of the day is flat across all three terciles at 0.68, 0.60 and 0.63, and Spearman between morning and rest-of-day is -0.074. The morning predicts the day only because it is part of it. It carries no information about what is left.

After 23:30 Taipei, the median day is finished

Median residual range after 23:30 is 0.0% in every period: on more than half of sessions no new extreme arrives after that. Mean residual after 02:30 is 2.9% to 4.0%, and a new daily extreme still appears on 23.7% to 26.7% of sessions — small but not zero, so this is a probability statement and not a curfew.

Range accumulates ahead of volume, so the profile is not simply session participation

Cumulative range runs 15.2 to 18.3 points ahead of cumulative volume through the day, and the per-slot share correlation between the two is only 0.40 to 0.53. The largest single divergence is the 07:00 session open, where range takes 10 to 14 points more share than volume does. Whatever produces the profile, 'more people are trading' is not a sufficient description of it.

The first null was wrong and would have produced a confident false finding

Permuting whole bars into different slots moves each bar's absolute price level with it, so a bar from the session high can land beside one from the session low and the entire range appears inside the first hour. That null completes 39.0% by 07:30 against an observed 20.0%, sits above the observed profile at every slot, and returns |z| up to 85. It is retained in results.json as a rejected null because the failure is instructive: a null must preserve the path, not just the ingredients.

Spearman on a cumulative curve is always 1.0 and proves nothing

Every cumulative profile is monotone, so any two of them rank-correlate perfectly. The first version of this study reported 1.0 for period stability, weekday stability, DST stability and the range-versus-volume screen, and all four numbers were vacuous. Every shape comparison here runs on per-slot increments instead, where the same four comparisons return 0.877 to 0.919, 0.914 to 0.966, 0.867 and 0.40 to 0.53.

Monday front-loads and Friday does not

Monday completes 45.3% of its range by 09:30 against 33.2% for Friday, with the same profile shape (increment Spearman 0.96 against all days). The weekend gap is absorbed in Monday's Asian morning. Mean daily range barely differs across weekdays ($64.79 to $70.50), so this is redistribution within the day rather than a busier day.

Limitations

  • Descriptive, not predictive, and never directional. The profile says how much range has accumulated, never which way price went or will go.
  • The level of the profile is not stationary. The Asian share has risen across the sample, so any fixed threshold read off the pooled profile will be wrong in the direction of under-stating the morning.
  • Taipei has no DST and the US session does, so the fixed-clock profile blurs the US block by one hour. The two regimes are reported separately for that reason.
  • The daily boundary is 07:00 Taipei. A different boundary redistributes the profile; the null is recomputed with the same boundary, so the excess is unaffected, but raw levels are not comparable across boundary choices.
  • Volume is TradingView spot tick volume, not exchange volume. RS-XAUUSD-20260818-003 established it rank-correlates 0.873 with real COMEX volume, which is adequate for a profile screen and not for a volume rule.
  • One instrument, 652 sessions, one strong uptrend.
  • No result changes formal S1 or S2 logic, live risk, or an entry checklist.

Method and evidence boundary

That the profile is real but that its usable form is narrower than it looks — specifically that a fixed Taipei clock is the wrong axis and that the level of the profile is not stationary.
