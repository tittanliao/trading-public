Key findings

No single Macro factor separates outcomes

No individual Macro factor separates outcomes in either strategy. Every observed gap (S1 1.08-5.85pp, S2 0.54-6.43pp) is far below what its own sample can resolve (S1 ~13.3-15.2pp, S2 ~23.2-26.4pp), and no noise test comes near significance.

The composite is half one input

The composite is half one input. US10Y and Real Rate agree 89.2% of the time in the S1 window and Real Rate is US10Y minus T10YIE by construction, so 3 of 6 points move together. It behaves closer to a four-factor score with one input at half weight.

The composite separates less than first reported

The replicated composite separates less than the original study reported: a 1.49pp verdict spread against 14.9pp resolvable (noise p=0.973), versus 3.98pp before. The difference is the stricter previous-day assignment used here.

S2 and GVZ is the only near-miss

S2 and GVZ is the only near-miss and the only result worth carrying forward. Below GVZ 22 S2 wins 59.72% (n=72, PF 3.421); above it 34.33% (n=67, PF 1.2), a 25.39pp gap that does exceed the 23.2pp resolution limit. It does NOT survive correction for having searched 15 thresholds: shuffling outcomes reproduces a best gap this large 10.7% of the time.

The rule threshold of 20 is not where any S1 signal is

The current rule threshold of 20 is not where any S1 signal is. At 20 the S1 gap is 1.94pp; what little gradient S1 shows sits at the low end, below 15-17, in the opposite direction to the rule that was removed.

DXY points the opposite way to the panel, in both strategies

DXY is the only factor pointing the same way in both strategies, and it points against the panel: the state the panel calls gold-bullish (dollar below its average) has the LOWER win rate in both (S1 55.88% vs 61.73%, S2 44.29% vs 50.72%). Not significant; recorded because a consistent wrong-way sign is more interesting than a random one.

Evidence limits

    Method and evidence boundary

    Expected negative. The composite's failure was a sample-size failure, and splitting the same trades by single factors cannot add power. GVZ was the one plausible exception because its rule claimed a mechanism (S2 mean reversion degrading in high volatility) rather than a correlation.
