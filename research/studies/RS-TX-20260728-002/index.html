TX annual Jan-open to Jul-1 rally, Fibonacci pullback re-entry

For each year TX rallies from January's open to July 1st, do the 0.618/0.5/0.382 Fibonacci retracement levels of that rally (entered independently when touched, exited the following March 31) show a meaningful win rate, given only ~13 candidate years exist in the available daily history?

Key findings

Sample size is inherently tiny (7-8 trades per level)

Only 13 candidate years exist in the available daily history (2013-2025); every level's 95% confidence interval spans roughly 30-50 percentage points. Read every win rate below as descriptive, not as a validated edge.

The 0.5 level shows the highest win rate in this small sample

n=8, 62.5% win rate, PF 3.557 -- but n=8 with a 95% CI of 30.57-86.32% means this is consistent with anywhere from a coin-flip to a strong edge.

0.382 (the shallowest, most-often-touched level) has the weakest win rate

n=8, 50.0% win rate -- shallower retracements trigger more often but, in this sample, land closer to a coin flip than the deeper levels.

Method and evidence boundary

This is an exploratory, low-sample-size seasonal-pattern test, not a validated edge; the small n (7-8 trades per level) means results should be read as descriptive, not decision-grade, until a materially longer or higher-frequency dataset is available.

level_price(ratio) = jul1_price - ratio * (jul1_price - jan_open); 0.382 is shallowest (closest to jul1_price), 0.618 is deepest (closest to jan_open)
