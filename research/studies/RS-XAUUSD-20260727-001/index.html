Key findings

Macro WAIT remained profitable

211 matched trades; WR 56.87%, PF 1.837. The evidence does not support a categorical S1 block; rank score is 0 (middle of the 3 verdicts), not negative.

NEUTRAL is the weakest Macro verdict under the rank-score method

NEUTRAL has the lowest win rate (53.66%) of the three verdicts despite the highest PF (1.963) and the smallest sample (82) — it ranks last (rank_score -1), reversing its prior +1.4 shrinkage-based ranking. Both methods are reading the same 82 trades; they disagree because they rank on different statistics (win rate vs shrunk average return).

Asia was not the weak V3.9 session

154 trades; WR 57.79%, PF 1.999. The inherited Asia-only observation rule is not supported; asia ranks best of the 3 scored sessions (rank_score +1).

Use the matching 30-minute slot

The operational score looks up the current HH:00/HH:30 entry slot's integer rank score (section 13.2), range -2...+2. 5 of 48 slots are low-sample (n<5) and score 0.

normal_sl is the dominant fail type, not immediate_loss

104 of 197 losses (52.8%) are a clean normal_sl stop-out; immediate_loss is 25.9% (51 trades). Descriptive context, not a live-impact mechanism.

Counter-trend (4H bearish) entries underperform

146 trades against a bearish 4H RSI state: WR 47.95% vs 60.2% for the 304 aligned trades. Descriptive context (section 5.1 item 8), not currently a live-impact mechanism.

Chronological holdout: flagged stable, not clearly degraded

70/30 holdout split by entry time: in-sample (2024-01-05 to 2025-11-18, n=315) WR 57.14% PF 1.886; held-out (2025-11-20 to 2026-07-10, n=135) WR 54.07% PF 1.832. Held-out win rate sits inside the in-sample 95% CI [51.62, 62.49] and held-out PF stays above 1.0, so the fixed rule (section 5.1 item 11) flags 'stable', not 'degraded'. This is a chronological-bucket check on one already-fixed backtest export, not a re-optimized walk-forward — see results.json method.temporal_stability_limitation before treating this as proof the strategy is not overfit.

2026Q2 was the weakest full quarter in the export, not 2026Q1

Quarterly win rate ranges 48.94%-62.96% across 11 quarters with no monotonic trend. 2026Q1 was the best quarter (n=55, WR 60.0%, PF 2.563); 2026Q2 was the weakest full quarter (n=47, WR 48.94%, PF 1.429), though still PF>1. The quarterly shape is worth cross-referencing against execution records before concluding the backtest edge itself changed: a live result that weakens while the backtest holds points at execution, not at the strategy.

Method and evidence boundary

Macro and 30-minute timing may change expectancy, but should not be assumed to be binary permissions without V3.9 evidence; any live adjustment must be small, integer, and always shown with its own sample size and confidence interval. The temporal-stability check does not retrain or re-tune the strategy; it only tests whether the already-fixed backtest's win rate/profit factor holds up in the most recent 30% of trades by entry time versus the earlier 70%.

Raw CSV and private decision conversations remain in trading-private. This public page contains reviewed aggregate results, charts, and the reproducible method only.
