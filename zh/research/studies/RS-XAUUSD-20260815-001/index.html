S1 V3.9 / S2 V3.2 — post-signal 1–2 bar confirmation

After a current S1 V3.9 or S2 V3.2 signal, does waiting for the next one or two completed 30-minute bars—and rejecting a setup when those bars break the signal-bar low—screen out losing trades?

Key findings

S1 shows a repeatable one-bar screening association

On the 165 trades overlapping the available 30-minute history, T+1 holding the signal-bar low raised original-outcome WR from 55.15% to 60.83% (n=120). In the latest chronological 30%, it raised WR from 48.0% to 57.14% (n=35), while filtering 11 losses and 4 wins.

A second wait bar did not improve the recent S1 holdout

Full-sample S1 T+1/T+2 hold reached 66.67% WR (n=99), but held-out WR was 54.84% (n=31), below the one-bar rule's 57.14%. The extra delay is not justified as a live rule by this screen.

S2 did not validate in the recent holdout

Full-overlap S2 rose from 43.08% to 46.0% after one-bar hold and 55.0% after two-bar hold, but the latest 20-trade segment stayed at 20.0% after one bar and fell to 15.38% after two bars. Treat the S2 result as unstable.

This is screening evidence, not delayed-entry backtest PnL

The selected groups keep each original TradingView outcome and exit. Exact entry-at-T+2/T+3 WR, PF, SL/TP behavior, and missed signals require a Pine replay; only 36.67% of S1 and 41.4% of S2 trades overlap the frozen 30-minute bars.

Impact on practice

No active policy change. Research/publication only for this study.

Evidence limits

  • 30m price history starts 2025-09-17, so only overlapping trades are matched.
  • The trade export contains filled trades, not every raw signal; filtered/in-position signals are absent.
  • Original outcomes and exits are retained. Exact delayed-entry WR/PF requires a Pine replay with revised entry timing.
  • Subgroup comparisons are descriptive and multiple rules are not authorization for parameter optimization.

Method and evidence boundary

For long setups, a break below the signal-bar low during T+1 is associated with a worse original strategy outcome; waiting through T+2 may improve screening but can also discard winners and worsen entry price.

All trading timestamps use Asia/Taipei. Raw CSV, source manifests, and private decision records are not published. This page contains reviewed aggregate results, one reproducible method, and pre-reviewed charts only.
