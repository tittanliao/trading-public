19/51 (37.3%) immediate_loss trades have overlapping 30m OHLCV+RSI price history, up from V3.4's 23.3%, but still not full-sample.

Overall n/WR/PF here (450 / 56.22% / 1.869) reproduce RS-XAUUSD-20260727-001 exactly, confirming this deeper report did not change the already-adopted S1 advisory-score evidence.
