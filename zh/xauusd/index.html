XAUUSD weekly outlook

W35 reversed. Price reached 4,697.07 on Tuesday - a new high for the rebound and effectively the target band both producers' prior-week reports had named - then fell away, and Friday alone dropped 148.42 points (-3.20%), the largest single-day fall in four weeks. The week closed at 4,453.67, only 3.2% up from the weekly low of 4,445.546, the weakest weekly close in a month. The dollar index rose over the same three days from 99.129 to a weekly high of 99.726, closing 99.677, so the fall lines up in time with dollar strength rather than with a purely technical unwind. Short-term momentum is now deeply oversold - 30m RSI 21.62, 1h RSI 22.37 - while the daily and weekly RSI are still neutral at 54.55 and 52.89. Price has lost the 4h 50 EMA at 4,547.46, but the daily 50 and 200 EMAs at 4,334.78 and 4,307.59 remain far below, so the daily-scale uptrend is not yet threatened. Positioning as of the 08-25 report date still shows managed money net long 144,747 contracts, up 3,099 on the week - but that cutoff falls before the sell-off, so it cannot show whether crowded longs have begun to unwind.
